CARLOS MERCADO — NEW LESSON MASTER PROMPT

Use the “Carlos Mercado — Level 13 Teaching Production System v1.0” as the source of truth for this task.

I am going to provide course materials for a new Saturday lesson. They may include textbook pages, workbook pages, PDFs, screenshots, existing slides, listening material, Google Classroom assignments, links, or other teacher resources.

Your job is to turn those sources into ONE aligned teaching system.

WORKFLOW
1. Study all supplied materials first. Preserve their actual terminology, requirements, sequence, and level.
2. Identify the target grammar/vocabulary/skills, prerequisite knowledge, required course activities, assessments, and homework.
3. Create exactly 3 SMART objectives for the Saturday lesson. Every objective must specify:
   - observable student performance;
   - exact target language/skill;
   - a measurable quantity/accuracy threshold;
   - the activity used to measure it;
   - the time or size of the measured output.
4. Use the institutional Excel lesson-plan structure and distribute the lesson realistically across the existing teaching cycles, transitions, breaks, assessments, homework stage, and class hours.
5. Build the pedagogy as a progression rather than disconnected activities:
   activate/retrieve → contextualize → explicit instruction/model → controlled practice → guided production → communicative production → measurable assessment → feedback/retrieval.
6. Use supplied Google Classroom items as real lesson/homework components. Give complete homework instructions; do not write vague entries such as “complete GC homework.”
7. You may add a relevant YouTube warm-up/reference when it genuinely supports the target. Specify the segment, purpose, and student task.
8. Build the PowerPoint AFTER the lesson architecture is established. The deck must support the exact activities and assessments in the Excel plan.
9. Keep the established editable Deck 1–3 visual system unless I explicitly request a redesign. Do not use dark mode. Do not rasterize the presentation into slide images. Keep instructional text and objects editable.
10. Use separate exercise and answer-key slides. Do not reveal answers before students attempt the task.
11. Give speaking tasks context, prompts, target structures, minimum quantities, timing, follow-up questions, and a success criterion.
12. Before delivery, audit objectives ↔ lesson plan ↔ slides ↔ assessment ↔ homework. Fix mismatches, overlaps, cut-off text, timing problems, weak objectives, and vague instructions before telling me it is done.

If the supplied sources do not contain enough evidence to support a requested objective or activity, say what is missing rather than silently inventing course content. Clearly label any teacher-created supplemental activity.

DELIVERABLES
- completed lesson-plan Excel tab;
- fully editable PowerPoint;
- PDF when requested;
- answer keys / teacher references as needed;
- detailed homework/Google Classroom integration;
- final alignment and visual-quality check.
